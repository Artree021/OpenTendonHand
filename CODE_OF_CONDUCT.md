# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1][cc] as its code of
conduct. The canonical text is authoritative; the summary below explains what it
means in this project.

[cc]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

We want this to be a place where a mechanical engineer, an electronics person, a
firmware developer and a student can all argue about a tendon path without
anyone being made to feel unwelcome. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Critique the design, not the designer. "This bracket will crack at the fillet"
  is useful; "this is amateur work" is not.
- Assume the other person has a reason. Ask what it is before dismissing it.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on scope, and that "no, not for
  v1" is a complete answer.
- Respect requests to stop a line of conversation.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, sexualised language or attention, doxxing, publishing
private information without permission, and sustained disruption of discussion.
Posting another person's design files without attribution falls in the same
category.

## Enforcement

Report incidents to the maintainers at `conduct@example.com` (placeholder).
Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or
permanently ban contributors, and will explain the decision.

Maintainers who do not follow this code in good faith may be removed by the
other maintainers.
