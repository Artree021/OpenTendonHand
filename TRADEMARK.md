# Trademark Policy

The name **OpenTendonHand** and the logo in `assets/logo/` identify this project
and distinguish the official design from forks.

## You may

- Use the name to refer to this project, its documentation and its designs —
  including in academic papers, blog posts and videos.
- State truthfully that your build is "based on OpenTendonHand".
- Redistribute unmodified copies of the design files under the project name.

## You may not

- Present a modified design as the official OpenTendonHand release.
- Use the name or logo for a commercial product in a way that implies
  endorsement by the project.
- Register the name as a trademark, domain, or organisation identifier.

## Forks

Forks are welcome and should carry their own name, optionally with the
qualifier "a fork of OpenTendonHand". Keeping the license notices intact is
required regardless of naming.

Questions: open an issue.
